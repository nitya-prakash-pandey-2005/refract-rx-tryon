# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

**Record:** screen capture of the app in Chrome at 1440 px, plus 10–15 s on a phone (installed PWA or mobile Chrome) for the "on device" requirement. No music (or royalty-free only). Speak slowly; the numbers on screen do the work.

**Before recording:** have a live profile ready (your own scan with your real Rx if you wear glasses, or the Lena sample profile). Bag empty. Close other tabs.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home: the animated lens hero cycles Showroom → −6.50 → +3.00 | "Every virtual try-on shows you frames without lenses. But if you wear glasses, your lenses change your face: minus lenses shrink your eyes, plus lenses magnify them, and strong lenses get thick at the edge. This is Refract: the first try-on that knows your prescription." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Scan: start the camera, show the live checks turning green, voice prompt, auto-capture. Then a phone shot of the same screen | "You scan with your glasses off. You can't see the screen without them, so Refract talks you through it and takes the photo automatically. Your iris, about 11.7 mm in nearly everyone, is the ruler for your pupillary distance." |
| 0:35–0:50 | Prescription: tap the "−6.50 strong" preset; the lens cross-section draws; −7% shows | "Enter your prescription and you see straight away what it does: at −6.5, your eyes look 7% smaller to everyone else." |
| 0:50–1:15 | Your face: click *Run YouCam analysis*; the Face Analyzer and Skin Analysis cards fill in; the units ledger shows the calls | "Now three YouCam APIs do the beauty work. **AI Face Analyzer** reads my face shape, eye shape, eyelids and colours. **AI Skin Analysis** scores the eye area: dark circles, eye bags, crow's feet. That's exactly the area a lens sits in front of." |
| 1:15–1:50 | Studio: Juno (big square) at Face zoom → toggle **Showroom / Your Rx** twice → switch to Eyes zoom → toggle again | "Here's the moment. This is the frame as every store shows it. And this is reality with my lenses: smaller eyes, the face cutting in at the edge, and power rings. It's computed per pixel from real lens optics, along my astigmatism axis." |
| 1:50–2:10 | Lens index chips: tap 1.50 → 1.67 → 1.74; read the note. Then the frame strip: tap Pico | "Refract picks the lens index for this frame, and it's honest: here the thinnest lens saves almost nothing, so it says don't pay for it. The frames are ranked for my prescription. Smaller frames keep strong lenses thin." |
| 2:10–2:30 | Look switch: Generic look → Rx-tuned look (YouCam **Makeup VTO** render) through the lenses | "Then the makeup. **YouCam Makeup VTO** renders a generic smoky eye, which disappears behind minus lenses, and a look Refract computed for my lens power: brighter lids, finer liner, lifted lashes." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Ask Refract: tap "I love big oversized frames…" and show the tool-call trace and buttons | "You can ask the agent. Every number it gives comes from a visible tool call to the optics engine. It suggests; you decide." |
| 2:45–2:58 | Bag order sheet → For retailers matrix | "You get a lab-ready order sheet. Retailers get a catalogue audit and a feed AI shopping agents can read. Refract: see yourself through your lenses before you buy." |

**On-screen captions to add:** "YouCam AI Face Analyzer", "YouCam AI Skin Analysis", "YouCam AI Makeup VTO" when each one appears.
